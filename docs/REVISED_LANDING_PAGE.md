# Case App Landing Page: "The Unified Profile Journey"

This document outlines the revised visual, copy, and interaction layout for the [caseshow.info](https://caseshow.info) landing page. 

It tells a single, continuous, morphing visual story: **cluttered documents and credentials morphing into a clean, mobile portfolio, which is then shared instantly to an employer's device, ending with a downloaded digital business card.**

---

## 🎨 Design System & Visual Grammar
- **Typography:**
  - **Display/Headers:** *Fraunces* (Serif) — for premium authority and credibility.
  - **Telemetry/Labels:** *IBM Plex Mono* (Monospace) — for fast-loading telemetry and data parameters.
  - **Body/System:** *Inter* (Sans-serif) — for high-readability copy.
- **Palette (Warm, Organic & Professional):**
  - **Background:** Paper (`#F1EDE2`) and Card (`#FBF9F2`)
  - **Primary Text:** Ink (`#20281F`) and Ink-Soft (`#57604F`)
  - **Active Accent:** Brass (`#A8783A`)
  - **Verified Status:** Green (`#4B6A52`)

---

## 📽️ The Continuous 8-Second Morphing Shot: "From Chaos to Card"

A single macro camera journey with **zero scene cuts**, flowing through four distinct visual stages of professional presentation.

```
[0.0s – 2.0s: The Chaos] ────────► [2.0s – 4.0s: The Morph] ──────► [4.0s – 6.0s: The Beam] ──────► [6.0s – 8.0s: The Handoff]
  Cluttered papers & PDFs           Consolidating into phone         Beaming link to new device       Downloading digital card
```

---

## 📖 Chapter Blueprint & Marketing Copy

### Chapter 01 | 0.0s – 2.0s: The Chaos of Documents (The Problem)
- **Visual Focus:** A floating cloud of disorganized professional documents (crumpled Word resumes, PDF certificates, screenshot images of past work, recommendation letters) drifting in 3D space. A clean scanning laser line sweeps over them.
- **Brand Copy:**
  - *Headline (Fraunces):* **Tired of the document chase?**
  - *Subcopy (Inter):* Sending multiple heavy PDFs, Google Drive links, and scattered screenshots over WhatsApp is chaotic. Most employers and clients just ignore them.
- **Mono Telemetry/HUD Overlay:**
  - `STATUS: RAW DOCUMENT STACK`
  - `FILE COUNT: 8 SCATTERED FILES`
  - `TOTAL SIZE: 24.8 MB (HEAVY)`
  - `OUTREACH FRICTION: HIGH`

---

### Chapter 02 | 2.0s – 4.0s: The Morph (The Solution - Case Profile)
- **Visual Focus:** The drifting papers and certificates fold and compress in slow motion, morphing into a single, clean, glowing smartphone screen. The screen lights up to display a beautifully organized public Case profile.
- **Brand Copy:**
  - *Headline (Fraunces):* **One link. Your entire professional world.**
  - *Subcopy (Inter):* Case packages your resume, certificates, video proof, and recommendations into one fast-loading, beautiful link. You stop chasing jobs—your work speaks for itself.
- **Mono Telemetry/HUD Overlay:**
  - `STATUS: CONSOLIDATED PROFILE`
  - `COMPACT LINK: caseshow.info/@user`
  - `COMPLETENESS SCORE: 98%`
  - `LOAD TIME: 0.08 SECONDS`

---

### Chapter 03 | 4.0s – 6.0s: The Beam (The Sharing & Convenience)
- **Visual Focus:** The camera pans dynamically as a stream of neon data (representing the link transfer) beams from the owner's phone screen across the space, entering another phone screen (the viewer/employer's device) which wakes up instantly with the profile loaded.
- **Brand Copy:**
  - *Headline (Fraunces):* **Share instantly. Load in milliseconds.**
  - *Subcopy (Inter):* Share your custom link once on WhatsApp, SMS, or email. It opens instantly on any device, anywhere, with zero download latency for the employer.
- **Mono Telemetry/HUD Overlay:**
  - `STATUS: INSTANT LINK TRANSFER`
  - `VIEWER ID: EMPLOYER_CONFIRMED`
  - `BANDWIDTH USAGE: ULTRA-LOW`
  - `INQUIRY GATEWAY: ACTIVE`

---

### Chapter 04 | 6.0s – 8.0s: The Handoff (The Booking & Business Card)
- **Visual Focus:** The camera zooms into the employer's screen showing the "Download Business Card" button being pressed. A premium digital business card slides down into their local device contacts.
- **Brand Copy:**
  - *Headline (Fraunces):* **Get hired on the spot.**
  - *Subcopy (Inter):* Give employers complete confidence to say "YES" immediately. Let them download your business card and save your contact info in one tap.
- **Mono Telemetry/HUD Overlay:**
  - `STATUS: CONTACT EXPORTED`
  - `CARD TYPE: DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD`
  - `TRUST RATING: VERIFIED [✔]`
  - `CTA STATUS: CLAIM FREE LINK`

## ⚡ The Spec Configurator & Rate Calculator

At the bottom of the scroll journey, an interactive dashboard tool converts this visual metaphor into business logic:
- **Inputs:** User selects their current showcase method (e.g., PDF Resume, Scattered WhatsApp Photos, Instagram page).
- **Calculated Physics Outputs:**
  - **Trust Score:** Displays how much client trust increases with Case (+150%).
  - **Friction Reduction:** Saves 5+ days of back-and-forth interview loops.
  - **Earning Potential:** Estimates how much more they can charge with verified proof of work.
